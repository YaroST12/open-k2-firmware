PD trigger (active) -

Requests a contract from the source. Boot is passive; the K2 refuses this while a live sink (laptop) is attached. Anything above 5 V asks for confirmation. Use a known charger and nothing valuable on the output.

Cable resistance -

Passive: uses your own load. Draw 1 A or more above an idle reading (under 0.2 A). The figure includes the K2's own path up to its VBUS tap.

PD black box -

Captures frozen on a contract change, hard reset, VBUS dip or detach: the seconds before and 0.5 s after. Wheel zooms, drag pans, double-click resets.
