## Hardware lessons from today
- The CUKTECH runs cable discovery only a few times, or only for a short window, after a full mains
  power-off. Power it off until both CC lines read 0 V before any spoof test.
- After `pd-reread --wait 0` a NEW run starts (it is not a status query).
- `k2_remote.py reboot` is the clean detach until the Rd-hold bug is fixed.
- The laptop's USB stack rebooted the whole system after many K2 replugs. Keep device sessions short
  and avoid rapid HID replugs.
