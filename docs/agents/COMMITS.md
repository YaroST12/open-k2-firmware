# Commit messages

1. **Header**: `<type>(<scope>): <summary>` (at most 72 characters, imperative mood).
2. **Body**:
   * **Summary**: what changed.
   * **Rationale**: the problem, what was measured or seen on the wire, and why this change.
   * **Details**: register names, bits and formulas where they matter.
3. **Trailer**: a `Co-Authored-By:` line naming the model that wrote the change, when one did.
