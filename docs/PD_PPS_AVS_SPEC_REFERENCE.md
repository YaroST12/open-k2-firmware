## 2. Timer Start & Stop Events (and Spec Ambiguities)

> **Direct answer to your main assumption:** **No, transition timers do NOT start at the `Accept` message.** Across USB PD R3.2 V1.2, R3.3 V1.0, PD3 CTS Q4 2026, and USB-C SPT V1.2, every Source/Sink transition timer (`PSTransitionTimer`, `tSrcTransition`, `tSrcTransReq`, `tPpsSrcTransSmall`, `tPpsSrcTransLarge`, `tAvsSrcTransSmall`, and `t0` via `tSrcTransition`) is anchored to **the last bit of the EOP of the `GoodCRC` message acknowledging the `Accept` message** (transmitted by the Sink / received by the Source).

### 2.2 Critical Ambiguities & Logger Design Implications

1. **Accept vs. GoodCRC(Accept):**  
   Your tester timestamps `Request`, `Accept`, and `PS_RDY` (presumably packet start or end). Because the normative timers start at the **end of the `GoodCRC` acknowledging `Accept`**, there is a physical gap between `Accept` and `GoodCRC(Accept)` (`tTransmit ≤ 195 µs` in Table 7.10 plus `Accept` and `GoodCRC` packet wire time), which can be larger if a `GoodCRC` retry occurs (`tRetry ≤ 195 µs`, `CRCReceiveTimer` `900..1100 µs`). Measuring `Accept -> PS_RDY` directly slightly *overestimates* elapsed Source transition time if `Accept` EOP is used instead of `GoodCRC(Accept)` EOP.
2. **Entering a PPS APDO (from Fixed/AVS or a different PPS APDO) vs. Stepping Within the Same PPS APDO:**  
   - **Within the same PPS APDO** (CV mode, same requested current): `tSrcTransition` (`25 ms`) does **not** apply (`Sec 4.5.2.1`, `Table 4.6`). Source starts immediately after `GoodCRC(Accept)` and finishes within `tPpsSrcTransSmall` (`25 ms`) or `tPpsSrcTransLarge` (`275 ms`).
   - **Switching into PPS from Fixed/AVS (or to a different APDO/PDO):** `Sec 4.1.3.2.1` states: `"If the Sink negotiates for a new PDO or APDO, then the transition between the current PDO or APDO and the new PDO or APDO Shall occur as described in Section 4.5."` And CTS `TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2#2` allows `tSrcTransReq` (**`325 ms` SPR / `760 ms` EPR**) for `PS_RDY` start when changing PDOs (`tSrcTransition ≥ 25 ms` wait + `tSrcReady ≤ 285 ms` SPR / `≤ 720 ms` EPR).
   - **Minor spec inconsistency in CTS vs SPT:** In `SPT.6`, even the initial Fixed `5V` → PPS `Vmin` switch checks `tPpsSrcTrans[Small/Large]` (`SPT.6.G.2.f.2`), whereas PD3 CTS `TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2` applies `tSrcReady`/`tSrcTransReq` (`325 ms`) to PDO transitions (`d.i`) and `tPpsTransSmall`/`tPpsTransLarge` (`25 ms`/`275 ms`) strictly to transitions *within* PPS APDOs (`d.ii`). For your compliance check, expect up to `325 ms` on the *first* Request entering a PPS APDO, and enforce `25 ms` / `275 ms` on steps within that APDO.
3. **Does `tPpsSrcTransSmall` (`25 ms`) apply to VBUS settle, `PS_RDY`, or both?**  
   - **PD R3.3 Figures 4.22 & 4.23** (`Sec 4.5.2.1`) draw the `tPpsSrcTransSmall, tPpsSrcTransLarge` span from `GoodCRC(Accept)` to `Send PS_RDY`, and **SPT V1.2 assertions `7.1.4.2.1#11/#12`** require **both** VBUS settling (`#2`) **and** `PS_RDY` transmission (`#11/#12`) within `tPpsSrcTransSmall` (`25 ms`) / `tPpsSrcTransLarge` (`275 ms`).
   - **However, PD3 CTS `TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2#9` vs `#10`** has a notable asymmetry: for `≤ vPpsSmallStep` (`#9`), it checks *VBUS settles* within `tPpsTransSmall` (`25 ms`) while the general `PS_RDY` check in `#2` uses `tSrcTransReq` (`325 ms`) — whereas for `> vPpsSmallStep` (`#10`), it explicitly checks *both* VBUS settles *and* `PS_RDY` is sent within `tPpsTransLarge` (`275 ms`), and **`TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2#12`** checks that the Source does **not** send `PS_RDY` *before* VBUS is within `vPpsNew`.
   - Note also **`TEST.PD.PROT.SRC3.8#3`** only checks `PS_RDY` within `tSrcTransReq` (`325 ms`) at the protocol layer.
4. **`SourcePPSCommTimer` subtle reset anchor (`TEST.PD.PROT.SRC3.8#1` vs `7.31.16.2`):**  
   Notice in `TEST.PD.PROT.SRC3.8#1` and `SRC3.9#2` that the compliance test measures `tPPSTimeout` (`12.0–15.0 s`) from the **GoodCRC sent by the Source in response to the Sink's `Request` Message** (not from `PS_RDY`)! In contrast, `Sec 7.31.16.2` says *"after receiving any Message that causes the Source to enter the PE_SRC_Ready State, the last bit of the corresponding GoodCRC Message EOP is transmitted"* — when PPS `PS_RDY` completes, the Source enters `PE_SRC_Ready` and restarts `SourcePPSCommTimer` (which sits between 0 and ~275 ms after `Request` GoodCRC; your 12–15 s window easily covers both). Wait — remember *any* PD message exchange in Ready state restarts `SourcePPSCommTimer` (`Sec 7.31.16.2` & `PE_SRC_Ready`), so to test `tPPSTimeout` you must stay completely silent on CC (no `Get_PPS_Status`, `Get_Status`, etc.) after the PPS transition completes!

## 5. Additional No-Load Sink-Side Checks for a PPS / AVS Source

Everything below can be tested directly on CC/VBUS with zero external load:

### 5.1 Out-of-Range Requests, Invalid Object Positions, Reject/Wait, and Hard Reset Rules

1. **Out-of-Range `Request` / `EPR_Request` (Below `Vmin`, Above `Vmax`, Over-Current, Over-Power):**
   - **PD Spec Rule (`Sec 7.9.2` SPR / `Sec 7.30.3` EPR):** `"The Reject Message Shall be sent when the Source is unable to meet the Request Message due to an Invalid Request. Requesting current or power that exceeds the advertised limits or requesting a PDO object position that was not advertised are examples of an Invalid Request."`
   - **RDO Voltage Bounds (`Table 6.21` PPS / `Table 6.22` AVS / CTS `COMMON.CHECK.PD.8`):** `Output Voltage` `"Shall be greater than or equal to the Minimum Voltage field and less than or equal to the Maximum Voltage field in the selected APDO"` (for SPR AVS: `[9.0–15.0] V` if `B9..0 == 0` in APDO else `[9.0–20.0] V`; plus AVS `Bits 10..9` `"Shall be set to 00b"`). Note: CTS `TEST.PD.PROT.SRC.4` explicitly checks `200%` over-current/power triggers **`Reject`**. By `Sec 7.9.2`, voltage outside `[Vmin, Vmax]` or non-100 mV AVS alignment is an invalid RDO field combination that should be rejected (`Reject`), though **note `unverified` gap**: no dedicated CTS test case in `pd3_cts.txt` specifically probes `< Vmin` / `> Vmax` voltage in PPS/AVS RDOs (CTS only probes `200%` current/power in `SRC.4` and invalid Object Position in `SRC.5`).
   - **Source must NOT clamp and `Accept` an out-of-range voltage** as valid compliance behavior, and **Sink must NOT repeat a `Reject`ed Request (`Sec 7.4.2` / `7.9.2`):** After receiving `Reject`, the Sink `"Shall Not repeat the same Request Message, using the same RDO, unless there has been a New Explicit Contract Negotiation, Data Role Swap, or Data Reset."` Sending a *different* valid RDO is allowed.
2. **Invalid Object Position (`TEST.PD.PROT.SRC.5`):**
   - Sending `Object Position = 0` or `Object Position > Num_Src_PDOs` (up to 7 in SPR) must elicit **`Reject`**, **not** `Ignore`, timeout, or `Hard Reset`.
3. **`Wait` Message Handling (`Sec 7.9.2.2`, CTS `SRC3.8`, `PS.SRC.2`):**
   - Valid only when an Explicit Contract already exists. If a Source responds with `Wait`, the Sink must wait at least **`tSinkRequest` (Min `100 ms`, Table 7.9/7.10)** before retrying the `Request`.
4. **EPR Mode Protocol Traps (`Sec 7.9.2`, `Sec 7.30.3`, `Sec 7.30.5`, CTS `TEST.PD.EPR.SRC3.8` & `SRC3.14`):**
   - Sending an SPR `Request` (instead of `EPR_Request`) while in EPR Mode → Source **`Shall initiate a Hard Reset`** within `tProtErrHardReset` (`≤ 15 ms`).
   - Sending an `EPR_Request` whose embedded `PDO` copy (bits 31..0 in Data Object 2) does not *exactly match* the PDO/APDO in `EPR_Source_Capabilities` at `Object Position` → Source **must issue Hard Reset** within `tProtErrHardReset` (`≤ 15 ms`) (`TEST.PD.EPR.SRC3.14#3`).
   - EPR Keep-Alive timeout (`TEST.PD.EPR.SRC3.13`): Failing to send any PD message or `EPR_Keep_Alive` must trigger Hard Reset strictly between **`tSourceEPRKeepAlive` Min (`750 ms`) and Max (`1000 ms`)**.
5. **PPS Keep-Alive Timer Lifecycle (`TEST.PD.PROT.SRC3.8`, `SRC3.9`, `SRC3.10`):**
   - **Deadline (`SRC3.8`):** Sending a keep-alive `Request` at `tPPSRequest` (`10 s`) must NOT encounter `SinkTxNG` and must NOT trigger a Hard Reset.
   - **Timeout (`SRC3.9`):** Silence after `Request` GoodCRC must trigger Hard Reset strictly between **`tPPSTimeout` Min (`12.0 s`) and Max (`15.0 s`)**. (Your `12–15 s` check matches Table 7.9/7.10 verbatim!)
   - **Timer Stopped on Exit to Fixed (`SRC3.10`):** After switching from PPS APDO back to Fixed `5 V` PDO, the Source must **stop** `SourcePPSCommTimer` and **must NOT** send a Hard Reset across `> tPPSTimeout max`.

### 5.2 Switching Between PDOs and APDOs (and Legacy PD 3.0 `Sec 7.3.18` Mapping)

- **Where `7.3.18` moved in PD R3.2 / R3.3:** USB-C SPT V1.2's assertion mapping (`p. 15`, `p. 17–20`) shows PD 3.0 Section **`7.3.18 Change the Source PDO or APDO`** was reorganized in PD R3.2/R3.3 into **Section `4.5` (Figures `4.16–4.24`), Section `4.2.3` (`Table 4.6 Conditions Requiring Sink Standby`), Section `4.1.3.1`, and Section `4.1.3.2.1` (`"If the Sink negotiates for a new PDO or APDO, then the transition between the current PDO or APDO and the new PDO or APDO Shall occur as described in Section 4.5"`)**.
- **Two distinct electrical regimes (`PD R3.3 V1.0 Table 4.6` + `Sec 4.5` + `CTS TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2`):**
  1. **Cross-PDO / Cross-APDO Transition** (e.g., Fixed `5V` → PPS, PPS APDO #A → PPS APDO #B, PPS → Fixed, Fixed → AVS, AVS → Fixed):
     - Follows **`Sec 4.5.1`**: Source waits **`tSrcTransition ≥ 25 ms`** after `GoodCRC(Accept)` before ramping VBUS (`TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2#3` verifies VBUS remains within `vNew` of the *starting* voltage during `0..25 ms`!).
     - VBUS settles to `vNew` within **`tSrcReady` (`285 ms` SPR / `720 ms` EPR)** from `t0`, and `PS_RDY` begins within **`tSrcTransReq` (`325 ms` SPR / `760 ms` EPR)** from `GoodCRC(Accept)`, and `PS_RDY` is **never sent before VBUS is within `vNew`** (`TEST.PD.PS.SRC.2#7`).
     - Positive/negative VBUS envelopes (`Sec 4.1.3.1`): must stay above `vValid(min)` of the *previous* contract and below `vValid(max)` of the *new* contract on increases; below `vValid(max)` of *previous* and above `vValid(min)` of *new* on decreases; never below `vSrcNeg` (`-0.3 V`).
     - Transitioning *from* a PPS APDO back to Fixed/AVS: `SourcePPSCommTimer` must stop (`SRC3.10`); in CL mode (though at no-load a Source is in CV), note `Table 4.6 fn. 1` advises Sink only switches out of PPS when in CV mode.
  2. **Within-Same-APDO Transition:**
     - **Within same PPS APDO (CV mode, current unchanged):** **No `tSrcTransition` 25 ms wait** (`Sec 4.5.2.1`); transitions immediately and obeys `tPpsSrcTransSmall` (`25 ms`, `≤ 500 mV`) / `tPpsSrcTransLarge` (`275 ms`, `> 500 mV`), `vPpsSlewPos/Neg` (`±30 mV/µs`), `vPpsNew` (`±5%`), `vPpsValid` (`±0.1 V` beyond `vPpsNew`!).
     - **Within same AVS APDO:** Small step (`≤ 1.0 V`) uses `tAvsSrcTransSmall` (`50 ms`), no `tSrcTransition` wait unless current decreases (`Fig 4.19`); large step (`> 1.0 V`) in PD R3.3 V1.0 uses `tSrcTransition ≥ 25 ms` + `tSrcReady` + `tSrcTransReq` (`325/760 ms`), `vAvsNew` (`±5%`), `vAvsValid` (`±0.5 V` beyond `vAvsNew`).

### 5.3 Source Capabilities / APDO Advertising & Pairing Checks (Zero Load)

1. **ECN `Disallow Source_cap PPS APDO without AVS APDO` (integrated into PD R3.3 V1.0):**  
   `"A Source Shall implement SPR AVS APDO when implementing SPR PPS APDO"` (note: a Source may *defer* advertising SPR AVS and/or PPS APDOs in its *first* `Source_Capabilities` until after it queries `Get_Sink_Cap_Extended` and sees Sink Modes bits; however, for PPS deferral `Sec 3.2.5.2` states if Sink replies `Not_Supported` to `Get_Sink_Cap_Extended`, the Source `"Should include PPS APDOs"` so legacy PPS sinks aren't deprived).
2. **PDO / APDO Ordering & Field Rules (`Sec 6.4.1.1`, `Table 6.13/6.15/6.16`, `CTS COMMON.CHECK.PD3.3`):**
   - SPR `Source_Capabilities` order: `5V Fixed` (slot 1) → remaining `Fixed` (increasing voltage) → `Battery` → `Variable` → **`SPR AVS`** (at most 1) → **`PPS`** APDOs (increasing minimum/nominal voltage).
   - If SPR offers `< 7` PDOs and enters EPR Mode, unused SPR slots up to index 7 in `EPR_Source_Capabilities` must be zero-filled (`0x00000000`), and EPR PDOs start at index 8 (Fixed 28/36/48V followed by at most 1 EPR AVS APDO in the last EPR slot).
   - In `SPR AVS` APDO (`Table 6.15`), `Maximum Current 15V` must equal the `15V Fixed PDO` Max Current, and `Maximum Current 20V` must equal the `20V Fixed PDO` Max Current (or `0` if 20 V is not supported).
3. **No-Load Voltage Regulation, Ripple, Slew & Status Consistency:**
   - **Static accuracy (`vPpsNew` / `vAvsNew`):** At no-load, the Source must still stay within **±5%** of requested RDO voltage (`"account for DC regulation accuracy, line regulation, load regulation, and output ripple"`, `Sec 4.1.2.1.1`) — note your 1 kHz / 3.5 mV INA226 measurement needs an averaging window after settle (`> 25 ms` / `> 275 ms` / `> 285 ms`) so noise doesn't cause false fails, while capturing per-sample excursions outside `vPpsValid` (`±5% ± 100 mV`!) or `vAvsValid` (`±5% ± 500 mV`).
   - **No-load excursion timing:** Under `< 60 mA` load, `tPpsTransient` and `tSrcTransient` allow **`150 ms`** (not `5 ms`) for excursions inside `vValid` (except `tAvsTransient`, which lists `5 ms` max with no `< 60 mA` row).
   - **PPSSDB vs. VBUS cross-check:** Query `Get_PPS_Status` at `Vmin`, `Vmid`, `Vmax` under no-load and verify `PPSSDB.Output_Voltage` matches measured VBUS within `±3%` (rounded to 20 mV) or `0xFFFF`, `Output_Current` ∈ `{0..3, 0xFF}`, and `OMF == 0` (`TEST.PD.PROT.SRC3.7`).
   - **Slew rate note for ~1 kHz sampling (`unverified` / measurement limitation):** With `vPpsSlewPos/Neg = ±30 mV/µs`, a 10 V step at max allowed slew takes ~333 µs, which is faster than a 1 ms INA226 sample period; therefore a 1 kHz ADC can catch slow/sluggish transitions violating settle deadlines, and can detect impossible jumps if sample-to-sample `ΔV / Δt > 30 mV/µs` (e.g. `> 30 V/ms`), though true sub-millisecond `dv/dt` spikes above `30 mV/µs` during <1 ms ramps require faster oscilloscope/ADC capture to prove compliance.
